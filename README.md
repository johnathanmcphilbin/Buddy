# Buddy

Hey im Johnny from Ireland and I made Buddy during my Hack Club gap year

Buddy is a small project where you teach your computer what your world looks like and then make it react to it

It can see things through your camera understand what is going on talk back and actually do something because of what it sees

Maybe it checks if you forgot your wallet

Maybe it sees a hand sign and turns the volume up

Maybe you make something way stranger

That is kind of the point

Build it your way and have fun with it
